## Selling a coin

You can sell any coin at any time, including one that lost its round. There is no lock-up, no waiting period and no extra fee on sells. A sale pays you in ETH or \$DOLL, whichever you choose, in one transaction.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the coin and the amount">
    Use the trade panel's toggle to sell back to ETH or to \$DOLL. You can also stop part-way and hold a coin higher up the chain instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the quote and set slippage">
    Your coin is sold into its parent, the coin before it, then the parent into its parent, and so on up to \$DOLL. If you chose ETH, the same transaction changes that \$DOLL to ETH. Slippage is how far the price may move before your trade is refused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    One transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you pay

A sale pays the same fee as a buy, once, on the way out. Each pool it passes through also keeps its small pool fee. If you stop at a coin still inside the chain and hold it, you skip the main fee and pay only the pool fees.

The fee on a sale is divided exactly like the fee on a buy, so selling also builds [buy walls](/the-flywheel) under earlier coins. Taking ETH back adds the fees of \$DOLL's own market, the same ones listed on [Buying](/buying#what-you-pay).

## Who is buying

The pool is. It holds the parent coin that buyers paid in, and it pays that coin back out as people sell. Nobody can withdraw any of it. Under the price, each chain coin's pool also holds buy walls built from fees, which take in selling as it comes.

A coin that lost its round keeps trading against the coin it was paired with. Its creator keeps the full creator share, the part of each fee paid to a coin's creator, on those trades.
