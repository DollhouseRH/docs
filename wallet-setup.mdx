## What you need

A wallet that supports custom EVM networks, and ETH on Robinhood Chain for trading and gas.

Robinhood Chain is an Arbitrum Orbit network. Gas is paid in ETH and blocks are about 100 milliseconds.

## Mainnet: chain 4663

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Network name | Robinhood Chain |
| Chain ID | `4663` |
| RPC URL | `https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com` |
| Currency symbol | ETH |
| Block explorer | `https://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com` |

<Warning>
**Dollhouse is not deployed on mainnet.** Nothing on chain 4663 is ours. Anything claiming to be Dollhouse on mainnet today is not. See [Contracts](/contracts) for the addresses that do exist.
</Warning>

## Testnet vs mainnet

**Testnet coins are worthless.** They exist so the mechanism can be exercised end to end. The entrance token on testnet is a placeholder called **MockDoll**; on mainnet, Dollhouse adopts the real **$DOLL** from Pons.

Some deploy-time numbers are deliberately different on testnet so they can be tested in an afternoon instead of a year:

| Setting | Testnet | Mainnet plan |
|---|---|---|
| Sunset delay | 1 hour | 7 days |
| Bond | a small amount of MockDoll, doubling every 4 links | a flat amount in $DOLL, set at deployment to roughly twenty dollars at launch, the same at every depth |
| Steward | the deployer address | a single cold Ledger signer |

The role-transfer delay is 7 days on both, because it is a constant in the contract rather than a setting.

## Getting $DOLL

Dollhouse does not sell \$DOLL directly. Before your first trade inside Dollhouse, buy \$DOLL with ETH on Pons; the site links there. Everything downstream, every candidate and every canonical coin, is bought and sold with \$DOLL, not with ETH.

## A safety note

Always add the network and check the chain ID yourself, and check any token address against the [Contracts](/contracts) page before trading. Fake copies of chain tokens are common: a genesis token on another launchpad on this chain had 13 pools listed, several of them decoys priced far above the real one with almost no liquidity behind them.
