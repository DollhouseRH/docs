description: "How creators, earlier coins and keepers earn on Dollhouse, and how to claim."
---

## Everything is paid from the 1% fee

There is one revenue source: the 1% charged when $DOLL crosses link one, in either direction. Nobody is paid from a treasury, an emission or a token unlock.

## Creators

**40% of the 1% fee** on your coin. That is \$4,000 for every \$1,000,000 of volume.

Paid in **$DOLL**. It accrues to a ledger and you claim it whenever you want, to any address you choose.

You keep earning fees whether your coin wins or loses its round.

The claim right is transferable. Transferring it moves the future stream only; anything already accrued stays claimable by you.

## The head coin's creator: the season cut

While your coin is the head of the chain, every candidate in the round below is priced in it.

You take **half of the creator share** on all attributed candidate trading in that round, 20% of the fee. That runs for the whole round, which is fifteen minutes at first and doubles every two rounds up to twelve hours, and stops when a new head is crowned.

This is why the first rounds were extended from ten minutes to fifteen, and why rounds get longer as the chain grows.

## The developer

**20% of the 1% fee**, in \$DOLL, uncapped and ongoing. At the modelled volumes that is about \$10,000 across 20 generations.

There is no other developer income and no pre-allocation. Early buyers get $DOLL at the Pons open like everyone else, and there is no insider bag on any candidate coin.

## Earlier coins in the chain

**20% of the fee** is split among every earlier coin, weighted so the oldest end gets the most, the middle gets the least, and $DOLL always gets exactly 2x the newest link.

This is **never paid as cash to anybody**. It is converted into parent tokens and locked as a permanent bid under the ancestor's pool.

## Keepers

The conversion above does not happen by itself. A volunteer, a keeper, supplies the parent tokens and is paid in \$DOLL at the chain's own prices plus a **1% bounty**, capped at 20% of the \$DOLL the call consumes. Anyone can be a keeper. The protocol itself never swaps.

## Winning creators, one more time

A winner's bond is refunded **in full**, at the amount actually posted. It is capital returned, not a reward.

Losers forfeit theirs into the locked buy support under link one.

## How to claim

Every payout above is **pull-based**: the contract holds it under your address and you call a claim function to move it. Nothing is ever pushed to you unasked, and nothing can be claimed by anyone else.
