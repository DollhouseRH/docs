## One trade, followed through

Everything anyone earns on Dollhouse comes from trading fees. Take someone who spends \$1,000,000 of \$DOLL on coin #3. The fee on that buy is \$10,000, and it is divided three ways:

| Who | Gets | How |
|---|---|---|
| The creator of #3 | \$4,000 | In \$DOLL, ready to claim |
| The chain | \$4,000 | Locked as [buy-side liquidity](/the-flywheel) under the coins before #3 |
| The developer | \$2,000 | In \$DOLL, ready to claim |

Selling #3 back pays the same fee and divides it the same way. The full fee schedule is on [Tokenomics](/tokenomics).

## Holders of earlier coins

A coin in the chain earns from every coin that comes after it, for as long as the chain trades. It gets a part of the chain share, the part of each fee locked into the chain, on every trade of every later coin. It gets an extra part on trades of its child, the coin directly below it.

All of it is placed as buy liquidity in the coin's own pool, just under its price. Holders can sell into it at any time. See [The chain](/the-chain).

## Creators

You earn the creator share, the part of each fee paid to a coin's creator, on every trade of your coin. It is paid in \$DOLL. During your coin's own round, you get half of it and the head coin's creator gets the other half. The head coin is the newest coin in the chain. After the round, you get all of it, whether your coin won or lost.

While your coin is the head, every candidate, a coin entered in the next round, is priced in your coin. You get half of the creator share on all of that round's candidate trades.

## Claiming

The creator and developer shares wait in the contract under your address. You call a claim function to move them to any address you choose. Your shares wait until you claim them, and only your address can claim them.

You can transfer your right to claim a creator share. A transfer moves only what is earned after it, and what has already built up stays yours to claim. The developer earns the developer share in \$DOLL.
