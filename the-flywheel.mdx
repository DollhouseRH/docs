---
title: "The flywheel"
---

Every trade on Dollhouse pays a 1% fee. 40% of it goes to the coin's creator, 40% is locked into the chain as buy walls under the coins that came before, and 20% goes to the developer. Each pool a trade passes through also keeps 0.075% of it, locked in a buy wall in the same way. Nothing placed in a buy wall can ever be withdrawn, so every coin in the chain keeps earning from every coin after it.

A buy wall is money placed as buy orders just under a coin's price that can never be withdrawn, so selling into it never empties it the way an order book does. Holders of that coin can sell into it at any time.

## The loop

Every coin in the chain is bought and sold with the coin before it, called its parent. \$DOLL sits at the top of the chain and is bought with ETH. When you buy coin #3, your \$DOLL buys #1, that #1 buys #2, and that #2 buys #3, all in one transaction. Each pool keeps the coin it was paid in, and nothing in a pool can be withdrawn.

This is why the loop feeds itself. More interest in a round means more buying of the head coin, the newest coin in the chain. More buying means more money entering through \$DOLL and bigger fees. Bigger fees build bigger buy walls under the coins before it.

The loop runs in four steps:

1. A buyer spends \$DOLL on a coin, and the purchase passes through every pool above that coin.
2. The trade pays the fee once, and each pool it passes through keeps a small cut.
3. The creator claims the creator share, and the chain share becomes buy walls under the earlier coins.
4. Each round adds a new coin below the head, and every trade of that coin pays into every coin above it, forever.

The full split of every fee is on [Tokenomics](/tokenomics).

## Buy walls, not cash

No part of the chain share, the part of each fee locked into the chain, is ever paid out as cash. It is spent on the coin a pool is priced in and placed as a buy wall just under the price. The Locker, the contract that holds every pool, has no way to remove it. Each buy wall stays in its pool for the life of the chain.

Every buy wall placed appears on the Treasury page of the app. Each entry links to its transaction, so anyone can check it.

## Rounds add coins to the loop

A round is a timed contest that adds the next coin to the chain. Every candidate, a coin entered in the round, is priced in the head coin. So buying a candidate also buys the head coin and every coin above it. During the round, the head coin's creator gets half of the creator share on every candidate trade.

The winner becomes the new head coin. From its first trade on, it pays into every coin above it. See [Rounds](/rounds).
