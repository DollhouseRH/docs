title: "The flywheel"
---

## TL;DR

Every round forces buyers to buy the current head coin, and 1% of everything that enters or leaves is split between the creator, the developer, every earlier coin, and the parent coin's own liquidity.

More attention on a round → more buying of the head coin → more ETH entering through \$DOLL → a bigger 1% pot → more paid to creators, to earlier coins as locked bids, and to the parent's liquidity → a deeper chain with more coins whose holders want the next round to be good.

## Step one: a round creates demand for the parent

Candidates are priced in the head coin. To buy any candidate you must hold the head coin. To hold the head coin you must buy it with the coin above it, and so on up to ETH.

A round of any real size absorbs a meaningful amount of the head coin, which in turn requires a larger amount of ETH to make its way into the chain, creating buy pressure on every coin in the chain.

## Step two: the fee split

Traders pay **1%** on the \$DOLL side of link one's pool, in both directions. 

That 1% is split, by a constant in the contract:

| Share | Who gets it | Form |
|---|---|---|
| **40%** | The coin's creator | \$DOLL, claimable |
| **20%** | The developer | \$DOLL, claimable |
| **20%** | Every earlier coin in the chain | Locked buy-side liquidity under those coins |
| **20%** | The immediate parent's liquidity | Locked buy-side liquidity under the parent |

**During a round**, the 40% creator share splits **50/50** between the candidate's creator and the creator of the head the round is priced in. After the round, a coin pays 100% of the creator share to its own creator.

Family-to-family hops pay a separate **7.5 basis point** pool fee, which goes into the parent's reinforcement budget. No 1% is charged there.

## Step three: ancestor payouts as permanent buy support

Payouts are spent buying parent tokens and locking them as a **bid underneath the ancestor's pool**: a standing buy order that can never be removed. \$DOLL is weighted highest: it always receives exactly **2× the newest link's share**, deployed as locked \$DOLL buy support under link one.

A maximum of 10% of a coin's entitlement can be drawn per 24 hours, and any single call can buy at most 2% of the active liquidity range. 

## How to verify a deployment

Every lock is transparent and recorded onchain.

The bid deployer emits four events, and each one is listed on the Treasury page on the app lists all of them with a link to the respective transaction.

## Step four: the season cut for the head

Holding the head is an income stream, not a title.

For the whole of the next round, half of the creator share on every attributed candidate trade goes to the head coin's creator. Rounds get longer as the chain matures, up to 12 hours, so a longer round is a longer season cut.

## Step five: the contest does not stop at the bell

A generation's share of the ancestor fees is settled by the round itself. Every time it is paid out, all of it is locked as bid liquidity **under the coin that won that round**, which is the coin every later generation is priced through, so the support lands where the whole chain trades against it.

That share stays locked under the winner permanently, whatever happens to its price afterwards, which gives the parent coin a reason to be bought **between** rounds and not only during them. See [Tokenomics](/tokenomics).
