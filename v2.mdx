## Why there is a V2

Dollhouse contracts have no owner, no upgrade path and no switch. That protects every holder from a rule being changed under them, and it also means a rule cannot be fixed in place. The first version proved the idea: real trading, real support for the first coins, and a chain that started growing. Two of its rules held the chain back, and both were fixed in stone.

- **Creating a coin required a bond priced in \$DOLL.** As \$DOLL rose, the bond rose with it, and the coins that should have become the second and third links were priced out before they were made.
- **The buyback burned \$DOLL directly.** A burn puts nothing under the coins already on the chain, so holding a chain coin did not benefit from the fees the chain earned.

Changing those rules needed a new set of contracts, and a new set of contracts needs a new genesis coin. V2 is that: a fresh \$DOLL and a chain that starts from zero on the rules below. The first version's token keeps trading on its own pool and is not part of V2.

## What changed

| Area | First version | V2 |
|---|---|---|
| Creating a coin | A bond in \$DOLL, returned or forfeited by the round | Free. Gas only, no bond |
| When a coin trades | At the round's synchronized start | The moment it is created |
| Round length | Fixed, hours long | Ten minutes from the first entry, growing with depth; the winner is final the moment the last score is in |
| Opening price | A fixed share of the parent's supply, so coins deep in the chain opened tiny | Set in ETH terms from \$DOLL's averaged price, never above half the parent |
| Where a coin trades | Any pool | Its own pool only, so no copy pool can undercut the fee |
| Buyback | Bought and burned \$DOLL | Buys the head coin through every link on the chain and burns it; round winners first |
| Art | Terminals often showed a blank avatar | Every coin's art resolves from its first trade |

Each of these has its own page: [Launching a coin](/launching-a-coin), [Rounds](/rounds), [Tokenomics](/tokenomics), [The chain](/the-chain), [The flywheel](/the-flywheel).

## What first-version holders receive

A tenth of the V2 supply was bought in the transaction that created the V2 token and sent to every wallet that held the first version's \$DOLL at the snapshot, in proportion to its holding, with a cap per wallet so no single holder dominates. The full allocation went out in a single transfer per wallet. Wallets can check their allocation at [dollhouse.markets/airdrop](https://dollhouse.markets/airdrop).

Nothing was taken from the first version. Its fees funded buybacks and burns on its chain, and part of them funded the first V2 coin.
