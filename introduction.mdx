## What Dollhouse is

Dollhouse is a flywheel/ponzinomics launchpad. Every new coin is bought and sold with the coin before it, not with ETH directly, so a buy drives value up the whole chain. Anyone can enter a coin into a timed round, and the coin that attracts the most sustained buying wins the next spot in the chain. 

## Why Dollhouse?

Dollhouse is a new and unique spin on the quote asset pairing meta, using it to create a symbiotic chain of assets that boost each other's liquidity and price. Instead of a traditional launchpad where tokens are only incentivized to care about their own success, Dollhouse drives a cooperative ecosystem where all coins and holders can win.

## Dollhouse for Traders

Dollhouse was created by trenchers, for trenchers. The genesis token \$DOLL has a completely fair and transparent launch, and all tokens launched on Dollhouse have similar promises:

**Fair Launches.** Every round starts new coins at the very bottom of their curve, with no private round and no team allocation. Dollhouse does not allow for configurable whitelist wallets.

**No LP pulls.** Every coin's supply goes into its pool at launch, locked forever, for anyone, including the developer.  so you can have confidence that every token you bid on Dollhouse will be as free from market manipulation as is possible on the platform last level.

## Dollhouse for Creators

**Permissionless.** Anyone can enter a coin.

**Creators keep 40% of the trading fee, forever.**

**Winning earns you a season.** While your coin is the head of the chain, every candidate is priced in it, pulling real buying your way, and you take half of the creator share on that trading.
