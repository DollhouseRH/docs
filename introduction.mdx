## What Dollhouse is

Dollhouse is a launchpad for new coins on Robinhood Chain.

Every new coin here is born inside the coin before it, like nesting dolls. Each coin is bought and sold with its parent coin, not with ETH directly, so the whole chain is held together by real liquidity. Anyone can enter a coin into a timed round, and the coin that attracts the most sustained buying wins the next spot in the chain. Rounds are fifteen minutes at first, doubling every two rounds up to twelve hours, and the score is the average market cap over the closing window (the last fifteen minutes of the round). See [how a round works](/how-a-round-works). Everything else (the fees, the schedule, the locked liquidity) is fixed in the contract before a single coin exists.

<Note>
Dollhouse is a meme-coin launchpad. Coins here can and do go to zero. Nothing on this site is a promise of profit. Read the [Risks](/risks) page before you spend anything.
</Note>

## The nesting-doll picture

Picture a set of nesting dolls.

The outermost doll is **$DOLL**: the entrance coin, adopted from Pons rather than launched here. Inside it sits coin #1. Inside #1 sits #2. Inside #2 sits #3, and so on.

To reach the doll in the middle, you open every doll on the way in. That is literally how trading works here: to buy coin #3 you get \$DOLL on Pons, then \$DOLL buys #1, #1 buys #2, and #2 buys #3. Your money passes through every coin above it.

That is the whole idea. A new coin does not sit next to the old ones. It sits **inside** them.

## Who it is for

**Traders** who want to buy quality coins early, and who like that every coin they buy also creates real demand for the coins above it.

**Creators** who want to launch a coin without paying a listing fee, without asking permission, and keeping 40% of the trading fee forever.

## The three sentences that matter most

Traders pay **1%**, once, only when money enters or leaves the chain, not on every hop, no matter how deep the chain gets.

Every coin's liquidity is **locked forever**; there is no function that can pull it out, for anyone, including the developer.

Winning a spot in the chain **guarantees nothing** about a coin's price, and the price of any coin here depends on the price of its parent.
