## What Dollhouse is

Dollhouse is a flywheel/ponzinomics launchpad. Every new coin is bought and sold with the coin before it, so each buy drives value up the whole chain. Anyone can enter a coin into a timed round, and the coin that attracts the most sustained buying wins the next spot in the chain.

The chain starts at \$DOLL, the genesis coin. Coin #1 is priced in \$DOLL, coin #2 in #1, and so on down to the newest coin, the head coin. Dollhouse was created by trenchers, for trenchers.

## Why Dollhouse?

Dollhouse is a new and unique spin on the quote asset pairing meta, using it to create a symbiotic chain of assets that boost each other's liquidity and price. Instead of a traditional launchpad where coins are only incentivized to care about their own success, Dollhouse drives a cooperative, PVE ecosystem where all coins and holders can win.

## Where the value goes

Every trade on Dollhouse pays a 1% fee, divided three ways: 40% to the traded coin's creator, 40% locked in the chain as bids (20% under every earlier coin, 20% under the coin's parent), and 20% to the developer. 80% of every fee stays in the chain. The creator and developer shares are claimable in \$DOLL. Each pool on the route also charges a 0.075% fee in its parent coin, and that fee is placed in full as a bid back into the chain.

Liquidity placed in a Dollhouse pool can never be withdrawn, by anyone. Every coin's full supply of 1,000,000,000 goes into its pool at launch, with no team allocation, no private round, no vesting and no extra fee on sells, so every coin starts at the bottom of its curve.
