## What Dollhouse is

Dollhouse is a launchpad for new coins on Robinhood Chain. Every new coin here is born inside the coin before it, like nesting dolls: each coin is bought and sold with its parent coin, not with ETH directly, so the whole chain is held together by real liquidity. Anyone can enter a coin into a timed round, and the coin that attracts the most sustained buying wins the next spot in the chain. Everything else (the fees, the schedule, the locked liquidity) is fixed in the contract before a single coin exists.

## What a trader gets

**You are early by design.** Every round starts new coins at the very bottom of their curve, with no private round and no team allocation. Buying a coin means buying its parent first, up to \$DOLL, so your money is a real trade on every pool in between.

**One fee, at the door.** You pay 1% once, when your \$DOLL enters the chain, and 1% once when it leaves. Moving between coins inside the chain costs only a small pool fee per hop; the 1% never repeats.

**The liquidity cannot be pulled.** Every coin's supply goes into its pool at launch, locked forever, for anyone, including the developer.

## What a creator gets

**Permissionless.** Anyone can enter a coin. There is no listing fee, no allowlist and no approval step.

**You keep 40% of the trading fee, forever.** The split is a constant in the contract and cannot be changed by anyone.

**Your coin keeps trading either way.** A coin that loses a round keeps its pool, keeps trading, and its creator keeps earning on it. It simply does not join the chain.

**Winning earns you a season.** While your coin is the head of the chain, every candidate is priced in it, pulling real buying your way, and you take half of the creator share on that trading.
