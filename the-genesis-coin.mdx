\$DOLL is created on Pons V2 and is Dollhouse's genesis token. The token is named Dollhouse, with the symbol \$DOLL and a supply of 1,000,000,000. 

## Every route starts in \$DOLL

Every coin in the chain is bought and sold through \$DOLL. A buy of any coin starts by spending \$DOLL into link one's pool, the pool between \$DOLL and coin #1. A sale of any coin ends by taking \$DOLL back out of it. Bonds to enter a round are paid in \$DOLL too.

The trading fee is charged and collected in \$DOLL. The creator and the developer claim their shares in \$DOLL. The chain share, the part of each fee that stays in the chain, remains in \$DOLL until it is placed as [buy liquidity](/the-flywheel). Separately, half of \$DOLL's own creator fees go to buying back \$DOLL.

## \$DOLL fee share

As the oldest link of the chain, \$DOLL gets a large part of the chain share on every trade of every coin. A trade of #1 sends the whole chain share to \$DOLL. \$DOLL's part shrinks as the chain grows, but it continues on every trade for as long as the chain trades. See [The chain](/the-chain) for how the chain share is divided.

## Locks/Value Accrual

Everything the chain sends back to \$DOLL ends up in one place: locked \$DOLL single-sided buy liquidity. \$DOLL's share of every 1% fee, coin #1's share, link one's own 0.075% fee and every bond forfeited by a losing candidate are all placed into that same buy liquidity. Liquidity is locked forever and helps support the token price.
