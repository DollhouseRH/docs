title: "$DOLL"
---

\$DOLL is created on Pons V2 and is the entrance to Dollhouse. The token is named Dollhouse, with the symbol \$DOLL and a supply of 1,000,000,000. Every other coin in the chain is priced, step by step, against \$DOLL.

\$DOLL's own market lives outside Dollhouse, locked by its own locker under its own rules. The Dollhouse contracts hold no \$DOLL of their own and cannot touch that market.

## Every route starts in \$DOLL

Every coin in the chain is bought and sold through \$DOLL. A buy of any coin starts by spending \$DOLL into link one's pool, the pool between \$DOLL and coin #1. A sale of any coin ends by taking \$DOLL back out of it. Bonds to enter a round are paid in \$DOLL too.

The trading fee is charged on that \$DOLL side, on buys and sells, and collected in \$DOLL. The creator and the developer claim their shares in \$DOLL. The chain share, the part of each fee that stays in the chain, remains in \$DOLL until it is placed as [buy walls](/the-flywheel). Separately, half of \$DOLL's own creator fees go to buying back \$DOLL.

## What is locked in \$DOLL

Everything the chain sends back to \$DOLL ends up in one place: a locked \$DOLL buy wall in link one's pool, just below the price of #1. \$DOLL's share of every 1% fee, coin #1's share, link one's own 0.075% fee and every bond forfeited by a losing candidate are all placed into that same buy wall. Nothing placed there can be withdrawn, so the buy wall only ever grows, and it moves only when holders of #1 sell into it.

## \$DOLL's part of every fee

\$DOLL sits at the oldest end of the chain, so it gets a part of the chain share on every trade of every coin. A trade of #1 sends the whole chain share to \$DOLL. \$DOLL's part shrinks as the chain grows, but it never disappears. See [The chain](/the-chain) for how the chain share is divided.
