---
title: "$DOLL"
---

\$DOLL is created on Pons V2 and is the entrance to Dollhouse. The token is named Dollhouse, with the symbol \$DOLL and a supply of 1,000,000,000, and it is link zero, the coin every other coin is ultimately priced against.

## Every route starts in \$DOLL

Every coin in the chain is bought and sold through \$DOLL. A buy of any coin, at any depth, starts by spending \$DOLL into link one's pool, and a sale of any coin ends by taking \$DOLL back out of it. Round demand follows the same path: a candidate is bought with the head coin, the head coin with its parent, and so on up to \$DOLL. Bonds to enter a round are posted in \$DOLL as well.

The 1% fee is charged on that \$DOLL side, in both directions, and is collected in \$DOLL. Only the developer share (20%) and the creator share (40%) are claimed out of the vault; the chain share (40%) stays in \$DOLL until it is placed as locked bids. Separately, half of \$DOLL's own creator fees are used for \$DOLL buybacks.

## What is locked in \$DOLL

Everything the chain sends back to \$DOLL ends up in one place: a locked \$DOLL bid in link one's pool, just below the price of #1. \$DOLL's share of every 1% fee, coin #1's share, link one's own 0.075% fee and every bond forfeited by a losing candidate are all placed into that same bid. Nothing placed there can be withdrawn, so the bid only ever grows, and it moves only when holders of #1 sell into it. Placement is paced at up to a tenth of what is waiting per day, so the bid builds steadily rather than in lumps.

## \$DOLL's part of every fee

\$DOLL sits at the oldest end of the weighting over earlier coins and always receives exactly 2x the newest earlier coin's weight. A trade of #1 sends the whole chain share, its 20% for earlier coins and its 20% for the parent, to \$DOLL, in the same link-one bid. For deeper coins, \$DOLL's part of the chain share's 20% for earlier coins is:

| Coin traded | Earlier coins | \$DOLL's part of the 20% |
|---|---|---|
| #2 | \$DOLL, #1 | 66.7% |
| #3 | \$DOLL to #2 | 57.1% |
| #6 | \$DOLL to #5 | 34.5% |
| #11 | \$DOLL to #10 | 20.2% |
| #21 | \$DOLL to #20 | 11.0% |

\$DOLL's own market lives outside Dollhouse, locked by its own locker under its own rules, and the Dollhouse contracts hold no allocation of \$DOLL and have no function that touches that pool.
