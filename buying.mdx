## Buying a coin

Connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain, pick a coin and pay in ETH. One transaction changes your ETH to \$DOLL and buys the coin, and the coin arrives in your wallet. If you already hold \$DOLL, the trade panel's toggle lets you pay in \$DOLL.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the coin">
    Any coin in the chain, or any candidate in a live round. Coins that lost past rounds can still be traded too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount in ETH">
    The app finds the path for you. Your ETH becomes \$DOLL, and the \$DOLL runs down the chain to the coin you picked. To pay in \$DOLL, switch the toggle and enter the amount in \$DOLL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the quote and set slippage">
    You see the fee and the amount you should receive before you confirm. Slippage is how far the price may move before your trade is refused. Set a minimum amount received, because a new pool charges a snipe tax in its first seconds; see the [FAQ](/faq).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    One transaction. Your payment passes through every pool on the way to your coin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you pay

Paying in ETH adds one step first. Your ETH is changed to \$DOLL in \$DOLL's own market, which charges its own fees. After that step, paying in ETH and paying in \$DOLL cost the same.

Your trade pays the Dollhouse fee once, on the \$DOLL side, however deep the coin sits in the chain. Each pool your trade passes through also keeps a small fee of its own. The full fee schedule is on [Tokenomics](/tokenomics).

The more pools your trade passes through, the more it moves the price, and the quote shows this before you confirm. If you pay for coin #2 or deeper with its parent coin, the coin before it, you skip the main fee and pay only the small pool fees.

Most of every fee stays in the chain as [buy liquidity](/the-flywheel) under earlier coins. Buying a candidate during a round also raises its score; see [Rounds](/rounds).

## What you own

You own an ordinary token in your wallet, and you can sell it at any time. Sells pay the same fee as buys. The coin trades in its pool against its parent coin. That pool holds the unsold part of the coin's supply plus the buy liquidity placed under it, and all of it stays in the pool for good.
