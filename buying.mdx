## Buying a coin

Connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain, pick a coin and pay in ETH. One transaction converts your ETH to \$DOLL and buys the coin, and the coin arrives in your wallet. If you already hold \$DOLL, the trade panel's toggle lets you pay in \$DOLL instead.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the coin">
    Any coin in the chain, or any candidate in a live round. Coins that lost past rounds stay tradable too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount in ETH">
    The router works out the path for you: your ETH becomes \$DOLL, and \$DOLL runs down the chain to the coin you asked for. To pay in \$DOLL, switch the toggle and enter the amount in \$DOLL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the quote and set slippage">
    You see the fee and the expected amount received before you confirm. Keep a minimum amount received set: a new candidate pool's first 3 seconds carry a snipe tax that starts at 99% and drops by about a third each second, reaching zero after 3 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    One transaction. Your payment crosses every pool on the way to your coin.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you pay

Paying in ETH adds one step before the chain: the conversion from ETH to \$DOLL in \$DOLL's own market, which carries that market's own fees, shown in the first row below. From there both ways of paying cost the same. The route pays the 1% fee once, on the \$DOLL side of link one's pool, however deep the coin is. Each pool on the route also charges a 0.075% fee in its parent coin, so buying #3 crosses three pools and pays the 0.075% fee three times, about 0.225% in total.

| Fee | Paying in ETH | Paying in \$DOLL |
|---|---|---|
| ETH to \$DOLL conversion | 1% pool fee plus 1% \$DOLL creator fee | None |
| Link one's pool | 1% fee, once, on the \$DOLL side | 1% fee, once, on the \$DOLL side |
| Each pool on the route | 0.075% fee in its parent coin | 0.075% fee in its parent coin |

Price impact grows with the number of pools on the route and is shown in the quote, and gas on Robinhood Chain is cheap. For a coin at #2 or deeper, paying with its parent coin does not cross link one and pays only the 0.075% fee in each pool.

The 1% fee is divided three ways: 40% to the traded coin's creator, 40% locked in the chain as bids (20% under every earlier coin, 20% under the coin's parent), and 20% to the developer, so 80% of every fee stays in the chain. The creator and developer shares are claimable in \$DOLL; see [The flywheel](/the-flywheel). Buying a candidate during a round also counts toward its score; see [Rounds](/rounds).

## What you own

An ordinary ERC-20 coin in your wallet, which you can sell at any time. There is no lock-up, no unlock schedule and no extra fee on sells of any coin. Its market is its pool against its parent coin; that pool holds the unsold part of the coin's supply plus the locked bids placed under it, and none of that liquidity can be withdrawn by anyone.
